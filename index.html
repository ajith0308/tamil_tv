      Add a channel

      Missing a channel? Paste its live stream link (usually ends in .m3u8). It's added under “My channels” and kept after reload.

        TV Guide (EPG)

        Leave empty to use the playlist's own guide (x-tvg-url) or the free India guide from epg.pw. Channels are matched by tvg-id or name.

        Public streams can be offline or geo-blocked; those show “No Signal”.
